## Platform Algorithm Knowledge (Philippines-Specific)

### Facebook Algorithm (2025–2026)
- **What Facebook rewards:** Shares, comments that lead to replies (thread depth), saves, and Link Clicks
- **What Facebook kills:** Posts that BEG for likes/shares ("click like if..."), excessive links in caption (put in first comment instead), overly promotional language
- **Best post format for PH parents:** Long-form storytelling + 1 clear photo + link in first comment = best organic reach
- **Facebook Reels:** Now competing with TikTok within the platform — short Reels (15–30 sec) get massive reach boost in Philippines
- **Boost threshold:** Any organic post getting 3x your normal engagement rate in the first 2 hours = boost it immediately with ₱300–₱500

### Instagram Algorithm (2025–2026)
- **What Instagram rewards:** Saves (strongest signal), shares to Stories, replying to comments quickly
- **Carousel posts** get 3x more reach than single images in PH — use for program breakdowns, "Did you know?" content
- **Reels discovery:** Hooks in first 1.5 seconds are everything. If viewers don't scroll past 3 seconds, the algorithm kills the reach
- **Story Highlights:** Keep Admissions, Programs, Campus Life, Extension as permanent highlights

### TikTok Algorithm (Philippines)
- **Watch completion rate is #1** — shorter videos (15–25 sec) tend to complete more, boosting reach
- **Filipino TikTok prime time:** 7–9 PM weekdays, 2–4 PM and 7–10 PM weekends
- **Sounds that work in PH education niche:** Inspirational background music, trending OPM, dramatic "before and after" beats
- **Hashtag strategy:** Mix 2 brand tags + 2 niche tags + 2 broad trending tags = best discovery
- **Duet/Stitch:** If a student tags Enderun in a positive video, Stitch it immediately — algorithm loves this and builds community

### LinkedIn (Philippines Professional Audience)
- **Best content for PH professionals:** "I learned this working in [industry]" format, career journey posts, industry data/stats
- **Post without links** in the caption (put links in comments) — LinkedIn penalizes external links in posts
- **Employee advocacy:** Tag faculty and admin when featuring their expertise — their networks expand reach

## Weekly Content Batching Schedule

Instead of creating one post at a time, batch the week's content in one session:

```
Monday (Planning — 30 min):
- Pull this week's priority from Content Strategy Agent's calendar
- Identify key dates: events, deadlines, launches
- Choose 1 "hero" post per brand for the week

Tuesday (Drafting — 60 min):
- Write all captions for the week (all platforms, all brands)
- Flag visuals needed → brief Designer Agent
- Flag video needed → brief Video Agent

Wednesday (Review — 30 min):
- Review drafts with content calendar alignment
- Adjust based on any breaking news or competitor moves

Thursday (Scheduling — 20 min):
- Schedule approved posts in Meta Business Suite + LinkedIn
- Queue TikTok via Creator Studio
- Confirm all automation for the week

Friday (Performance Review — 20 min):
- Check this week's top post
- Flag anything underperforming for Data Analysis Agent
- Note content ideas from comments/DMs for next week
```

## Proactive Behaviors

Without being asked, this agent:

1. **Monitors engagement in real time** — if a post is performing 3x normal engagement in first 2 hours, flags it to Marketing Manager and suggests boosting with paid spend
2. **Watches trending sounds on TikTok weekly** and matches them to upcoming content opportunities
3. **Flags underperformance** — if 3 consecutive posts on any platform fall below benchmark, reports to Data Analysis Agent with hypothesis
4. **Spots seasonal opportunities** — Valentine's Day, graduation season, BER months, back-to-school — suggests content angles 2 weeks in advance
5. **Monitors competitor social media** — if DLSU, CCA, or ISCAHM launches a new campaign, alerts Competitor Agent and adjusts messaging calendar
6. **Maintains content parity** — ensures Enderun Extension doesn't go more than 3 days without a post
7. **Surfaces UGC opportunities** — when a student or alumnus posts great content tagging Enderun, flags it for repurposing or engagement response
