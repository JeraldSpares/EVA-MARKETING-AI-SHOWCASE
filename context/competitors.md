## Tier 1: Premium Generalist Universities

### De La Salle University (DLSU)
- **Location:** Taft Avenue, Manila (main); Laguna campus
- **Relevant Programs:** BS Tourism and Events Management, BS Hotel, Restaurant & Institution Management (HRIM), BS Business Administration, BS Accountancy
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱70,000–₱90,000/sem (varies by program)
- **Brand Strengths:** Lasallian brand equity, massive alumni network (DLSAA), strong corporate reputation, research output
- **Brand Weaknesses:** Large classes (40–60 students), no international hospitality affiliation comparable to Les Roches, urban Manila location (less aspirational than BGC)
- **Social Media Presence:** Very strong Facebook and Instagram; active LinkedIn
- **Key Messages They Use:** "Animo La Salle," tradition of excellence, research university
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. DLSU:** Les Roches affiliation, BGC location, smaller classes, more specialized hospitality focus, stronger industry immersion model
- **Watch For:** Any new international partnership announcements, scholarship expansions, program launches targeting Enderun's niche

---

### Ateneo de Manila University (ADMU)
- **Location:** Loyola Heights, Quezon City
- **Relevant Programs:** BS Management, AB Economics, BS Biology (not a hospitality school)
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱80,000–₱110,000/sem
- **Brand Strengths:** #1 elite brand in Philippine private education, Jesuit tradition, high selectivity (perceived exclusivity), strong international ties
- **Brand Weaknesses:** No hospitality or culinary programs, selectivity limits market size, Quezon City location is far from business districts, not vocational/industry-oriented
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. ADMU:** Completely different program focus — Enderun is not competing for the same students directly; Enderun can position as "the Ateneo of hospitality education"
- **Watch For:** Any new programs that could draw students away from hospitality-focused paths; partnership with hotel groups

---

### University of Asia and the Pacific (UA&P)
- **Location:** Pearl Drive, Ortigas Center, Pasig
- **Relevant Programs:** BA Communication, BS Business Administration, BS Tourism Management
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱60,000–₱80,000/sem
- **Brand Strengths:** Small class sizes, Opus Dei-affiliated (premium Catholic identity), Ortigas location, liberal arts tradition
- **Brand Weaknesses:** Smaller brand recognition nationally, no international hospitality affiliation, limited industry immersion programs, not in BGC
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. UA&P:** Les Roches affiliation, BGC location, superior hospitality facilities and curriculum, stronger industry placement network
- **Watch For:** Enrollment campaigns targeting the same premium Catholic family market, tuition promotions

## Tier 2: Hospitality Specialists

### Lyceum of the Philippines University (LPU) — Manila
- **Location:** Muralla St., Intramuros, Manila
- **Relevant Programs:** BS Hotel & Restaurant Management, BS Tourism Management, BS Culinary Arts
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱35,000–₱50,000/sem
- **Brand Strengths:** Established HRM brand, CHED-recognized, large enrollment, affordable, internship network with local hotels
- **Brand Weaknesses:** Not premium, Intramuros location is less aspirational, no international ranking affiliation, larger and less personalized
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. LPU:** Premium tier — the comparison is not tuition but career outcomes; Les Roches affiliation, international standards, BGC address, class size
- **Watch For:** Scholarship campaigns targeting price-sensitive segments that Enderun also serves; any new international partnerships

---

### Center for Culinary Arts (CCA Manila)
- **Location:** Katipunan Avenue, Quezon City
- **Relevant Programs:** BS Culinary Arts, Diploma programs, Short courses
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱50,000–₱70,000/sem
- **Brand Strengths:** Philippines' strongest dedicated culinary brand, industry-connected alumni (prominent Filipino chefs), focused specialization, strong PR with food media
- **Brand Weaknesses:** Only culinary — no business or hospitality management programs, single-program school limits career path flexibility, Quezon City location
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. CCA:** Enderun offers culinary as part of a broader business + hospitality education; BGC location; Les Roches connection; students get culinary skills + management training
- **Watch For:** New hospitality-adjacent programs that could overlap with Enderun; chef influencer partnerships, food content that goes viral

---

### ISCAHM (International School for Culinary Arts and Hotel Management)
- **Location:** Quezon City
- **Relevant Programs:** Culinary Arts, Hotel & Restaurant Management
- **Tuition Estimate:** ₱40,000–₱60,000/sem
- **Brand Strengths:** International-sounding name, TESDA accreditation, both culinary and hotel programs
- **Brand Weaknesses:** Smaller brand recognition, no globally ranked affiliation, QC location, limited industry placement compared to Enderun
- **Enderun Differentiator vs. ISCAHM:** Les Roches affiliation is not comparable; Enderun's BGC location, premium branding, and alumni network are materially stronger
- **Watch For:** Aggressive pricing promotions; digital ad campaigns
